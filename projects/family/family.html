Hotel Transylvania

  • Count Dracula and Mavis: Count Dracula is an overprotective vampire father who built the human-free hotel to keep his daughter, Mavis Dracula, safe after his wife Martha was killed by a human mob.
  • Mavis and Johnny: Mavis falls in love with and marries Jonathan Loughran (Johnny), a relaxed human backpacker, and they have a half-human, half-vampire son named Dennis.
  • Hotel Transylvania is a hidden, five-stake monster resort built by Count Dracula in 1897 as a high-tech, human-free fortress to protect his daughter, Mavis, featuring supernatural amenities like living suits of armor and talking shrunken heads before eventually opening its doors to human guests.

Count Dracula

In the Hotel Transylvania universe, the character's full name is officially Count Dracula (commonly nicknamed Drac). The franchise does not give him a separate human first name, but his character profile reveals that his full birth name is Count Dracula Vladislav (or Count Dracula of Transylvania), with "Dracula" serving as his family's surname.

© Sony Picturesreference

Mavis

In the Hotel Transylvania universe, her full name is Mavis Dracula. Just like her father, she does not have a separate human first name, so "Mavis" is her given first name and "Dracula" is her family surname. After marrying her human husband, Johnny, her name technically becomes Mavis Dracula-Loughran.

© Sony Picturesreference

Johnny

In the Hotel Transylvania universe, his full name is Jonathan Loughran (commonly called Johnny). Unlike the vampires, he is a regular human, so "Jonathan" is his first name and "Loughran" is his family surname. After marrying Mavis, he becomes a member of the monster family and is occasionally referred to as Johnny Dracula by Drac.

© Sony Picturesreference

Dennis

In the Hotel Transylvania universe, his full name is Dennis Loughran (often called Denisovich by his grandfather, Count Dracula). Because his father is human and his mother is a vampire, he is a half-human, half-vampire hybrid who carries his father's human family surname.
