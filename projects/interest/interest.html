Playing Badminton

Playing badminton is an enjoyable interest that combines exercise, focus, and skill. It requires quick movement, good coordination, and the ability to react quickly to an opponent's shots. Badminton can also be a fun way to spend time with friends while improving physical fitness.

Buying Ice Cream

Buying ice cream is a simple interest that brings enjoyment and relaxation. There are many flavors and toppings to choose from, so each experience can be different. It is especially enjoyable on a warm day or when sharing a sweet treat with family and friends.

Staring at the Stars

Staring at the stars is a peaceful interest that allows people to appreciate the beauty of the night sky. It can create a sense of wonder and encourage thoughts about space, nature, and the vastness of the universe. Watching the stars can also help someone relax and enjoy a quiet moment away from daily responsibilities.

what is absolute link and relative link?
→ Absolute link uses the complete url, it has protocol and domain, but relative link uses a path of directory relative to the file's location.

An example of relative link: personal.html, in html code, we put href="../personal.html" to help the browser navigate the intended file.

An example of absolute link: personal.html, in html code, we put href="https://beetle21-ar.github.io/cs140aria/projects/personal.html" to navigate the browser to the page.

Both work the same even though both have different type of link to do!
