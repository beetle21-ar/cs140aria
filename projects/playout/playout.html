Welcome to Oahu

Oahu is famous for its beaches, well-known surf spots on the North Shore. millions of people come every year to experience the beauty of the island. The ocean is a central part of life on the island. I love Oahu for my experiences here.

A short drive from the beach leads to Diamond Head, a historic volcanic crater. The trail inside the crater takes hikers up steep stairs and through military tunnels to a summit view. Standing at the top and looking out over the island makes it easy to see why Oahu remains such a memorable place to visit or live.
